# rob the bank — Story and Sandbox

Open `Assets/Scenes/FacilityPrototype.unity` and press Play, or launch `Builds/FacilityPrototype/FacilityPrototype.exe` with its adjacent files. Start opens Story and Sandbox. Sandbox keeps the interconnected facility and Tab character browser, and supplies all four weapons.

Story opens `Assets/Scenes/StoryLevel01.unity`: Night Deposit. Open the car's trunk with E and collect the pistol, purchased weapons, and magnetic drill together. Enter the bank. The first connected attack on the receptionist triggers the alarm, alerts every guard, and starts police reinforcements. Follow the security passage, install the vault drill, and defend it for 45 seconds. Retrieve **both the cash and gold**, then return to the car. Open the left-hand driver door, look at the driver's seat inside, and press **E** to get in and leave. Either loot order works; either missing pile prevents extraction.

Successful extraction pays **$2,500 total**. Cash accumulates across attempts and app launches. Failed attempts and restarts preserve the saved balance and purchased weapons. Payouts and purchases save immediately. A completed run cannot pay twice.

Open **SHOP / $balance** from the title, mode, pause, or completion menu to check your account and purchase permanent weapons. The wallet stays off the gameplay HUD. The pistol is already owned, the pump shotgun costs **$5,000**, and the AK-47 costs **$10,000**. Collect purchased weapons with the drill from the car. Purchases made after collecting the equipment are supplied on the next attempt.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / mouse | Move / look |
| Left click | Fire or melee |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4, mouse wheel | Select available weapons |
| Hold left click with AK-47 | Automatic fire |
| R with AK-47 | Reload the 30-round magazine |
| E | Open/close a door, collect equipment/loot, install the drill, or enter the driver's seat to leave |
| Escape | Pause / resume, or close the shop |
| Tab | Character browser, Sandbox only |
