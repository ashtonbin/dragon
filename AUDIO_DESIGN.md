# Facility horror audio

The current exploration scene and its live observer/zombie spawn prefabs use the new sound pass. Press Play, then Start as usual. No lighting or navigation rebake is needed.

- The old global ventilation and local duct hiss are replaced by a seamless twenty-second low drone with slow beating and dark resonance. The global source is `Low disturbing hum` at volume 0.14; two very quiet positional resonances add variation. The local pump vibration and occasional metal contraction remain. The loops fade in over 0.8 seconds at scene startup, avoiding an onset click without adding a dip to each loop seam.
- The shotgun uses `Shotgun Deep Concussion V4`: a sharp attack, sustained low pressure and dark concrete reflections, followed by a short tail. The report has -2 dBFS peak headroom and silent endpoints. It is shaped for weight rather than a long burst of bright noise.
- The pump action uses a real PA08 mechanical recording, replacing the old synthetic percussion. Playback starts with the rearward stroke at 0.24 seconds after firing; its rear stop lands at 0.43 seconds and forward lock at 0.76 seconds. The 0.69-second recording plays once at native speed, with source volume 0.55. Shell ejection remains at 0.40 seconds and the firing interval remains 0.95 seconds.
- Zombies have three recorded moans. One reusable spatial source plays them at volume 0.13, fading out with distance by 14 m. Initial delays are staggered, then there are 5-11 seconds of quiet between phrases. Moans pause with gameplay and stop on injury, knockdown, recovery, death or player death. Zombie pain uses a separate short recorded groan.
- The observer uses recorded adult pain screams when shot. A new shell interrupts the previous scream; individual pellets cannot stack screams. Corpse hits do not trigger another voice. The source follows the physical chest through knockback and ragdoll motion. Its volume is 0.66 with a 24 m spatial range.

Voice recordings are CC0; sources and edits are documented in [AUDIO_CREDITS.md](AUDIO_CREDITS.md). Authored WAVs and import settings are saved as project assets. The raw CC0 source downloads and conversion scripts remain under `.codex`, outside the game build.

`Tools > Facility > Apply Horror Audio` first saves an `AudioRecovery_*.unity` copy, then updates audio wiring in the current scene and backs up/updates the two live spawn prefabs. It preserves geometry, baked lighting, navigation, player settings, menus and character art. Future facility/weapon/character authoring also assigns the new clips. No audio or environment resources are synthesized automatically in Play mode.

`Tools > Facility > Replace Shotgun Pump Sound` applies only the recorded pump and its source mix, saving a `PumpAudioRecovery_*.unity` copy first.

For tuning, edit AudioSources under `Facility Exploration/Lighting and Sound`, the shotgun's report source, or `Body/Chest/Character voice` and `Quiet zombie moans` on the character. Change the live spawn prefabs too when changing character source levels. Saved mixes have separate gains for background, weapon, moans and screams.

The explicit standalone check `-facilityAudioTest <absolute output directory>` exercises the ordinary Start/spawn/pause paths and actual shotgun hits. It verifies saved clips, decoded waveforms, ambient playback, zombie moans and death silence, observer screams, corpse silence and audio error logs. It never runs in ordinary play. Current receipt and log are in `.codex/audio-final`; signal analysis is `.codex/audio-quality.json`, and installation/build/reopen receipts use `.codex/audio-*.json`.
