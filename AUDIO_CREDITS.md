## Editing and provenance

The observer samples were taken from the publicly available
[high-quality preview MP3](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/556/556976_12497831-hq.mp3)
of the CC0 recording. They retain the original adult male performance. Source
segments: 0.58–3.09 seconds and 3.93–6.48 seconds.

The zombie samples were taken from the creator's original
[OGG download](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/darsycho__zombie-moans_0.ogg).
Source segments: 0.17–3.14 seconds, 3.60–6.35 seconds, and 6.57–9.10 seconds.
The short zombie hurt/death groan uses 10.35–11.10 seconds (0.75 seconds), with a
3.2 kHz low-pass filter to retain a restrained, low vocal character.

All six samples are converted to mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Edits remove DC,
reduce rumble, apply short boundary fades, and normalize true peaks to -3 dBFS.
Zombie samples have a restrained low-pass filter to soften hiss. Playback levels
and spatial attenuation are controlled separately in Unity.

The reproducible conversion script is `.codex/audio-tools/import_voices.py`;
its report with durations, levels, source segment times, and SHA-256 hashes is
`.codex/audio-sources/voice-import-report.json`. Unused audition downloads under
`.codex/audio-sources` are not Unity assets and are not included in the game build.

The facility hum and shotgun firing report are generated by project code.

## Shotgun pump mechanism

`Assets/Audio/Weapons/Heavy Shotgun Pump.wav` uses **Pump Action Shotgun Reload**
by **AugustSandberg**, a recording of a Weatherby PA08 pump action shotgun.
The [creator's Freesound page](https://freesound.org/people/AugustSandberg/sounds/508746/)
releases the recording under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The asset is derived from the publicly available
[high-quality preview MP3](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/508/508746_1934171-hq.mp3).

Source excerpts 15.72-16.10 seconds and 16.21-16.52 seconds retain the final
backward and forward pump strokes, with only the quiet gap shortened. Processing
converts to mono 44.1 kHz/16-bit PCM, removes DC and sub-55 Hz rumble, gently
softens frequencies above 6 kHz, and modestly emphasizes the existing resonance
below 1.4 kHz. The original recording speed is retained, with short boundary
fades and a -3 dBFS true peak. No synthesized thumps, percussion, hiss, or firing
sounds are added. The resulting clip is approximately 0.690 seconds long. The
rear hard stop and forward locking transient occur near 0.190 and 0.520 seconds
into the clip.

Reproduce with `.codex/audio-tools/import_shotgun_pump.py`; exact levels and
SHA-256 hashes are saved in `.codex/audio-sources/shotgun-pump-import-report.json`.
